Overview

This article is aimed at easing migration of projects from CRYENGINE 5.2 to CRYENGINE 5.3 It will focus on changes made to the CRYENGINE's source code format.

CRYENGINE 5.3:

CRYENGINE 5.3 development focused on adding new features many requested by the CRYENGINE community, plus general bug fixes and other incremental improvements to the Engine.
